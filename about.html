Websites for local businesses, built properly

NEMORA is a small, independent web design studio in Stoke-on-Trent. We design and build every site ourselves, for comedy clubs, event companies, tradespeople and venues, and we look after them once they’re live.

Why we started NEMORA

Too many good local businesses are let down by their websites: slow, dated, hard to use on a phone, or impossible to update without ringing someone. Customers notice, and they go elsewhere.

We started NEMORA to fix that with sites that look as good as the business really is, a fixed price you can see before you call, and an admin area simple enough to update from your phone.

What we’ve built so far

Our recent work includes Spitfire Comedy, a stand-up comedy club in Stoke-on-Trent, Staffordshire Spiritual Events, which runs mediumship evenings across the county, and Law and Son Decorators in Wolverhampton.
