Good questions

Why do you survey before quoting?

Two homes with the same number of bedrooms can need very different moves. Seeing what is actually going means the quote is accurate, and the day runs to plan.

Can I get a price over the phone?

We are always happy to talk your move through on the phone, but your quote comes after the survey. That way the price is right first time.

What is the deposit for?

The 15% deposit confirms your booking and holds your moving date, so the van and team are reserved for you.

Do you only move people locally?

No. We are based around Hinckley and Earl Shilton, and we cover all areas of the UK.
