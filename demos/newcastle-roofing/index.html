Repair first. Replace when it's needed.

Most leaks start somewhere small. Before anyone talks about a new roof, these are the parts we check.

Tiles

Slipped, cracked or missing. Often one or two is all it takes.

Lead flashing

Where the roof meets a chimney or wall. A common cause of leaks.

Soakers

The lead pieces under the tiles along a wall. When they go, water runs in.

Cement

Ridge and verge cement cracks and drops out. We take it out and redo it.

Gutters

Blocked or leaking gutters push water where it shouldn't go.

Barge boards

Along the gable edge. We'll source ones that match the originals.
