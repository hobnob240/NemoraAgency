01Inspection

What we check before anyone says “new roof”.

A roof is a lot of small parts. When one fails, water finds its way in. These are the usual suspects, and each one is a repair, not a replacement.

  1. Slipped or cracked tiles

    One or two out of place is often all it takes. Replaced and matched.

  2. Lead flashing

    Where the roof meets a chimney or wall. Worn lead is one of the most common causes of a leak.

  3. Soakers

    The small pieces of lead tucked under the tiles along a wall. When they go, water runs inside.

  4. Old cement

    Ridge and verge cement cracks and drops out over the years. We take it out and redo it.

  5. Gutters

    Blocked or leaking gutters push water where it shouldn't go. Repaired or replaced.

  6. Barge boards

    The boards along the gable edge. We'll source ones that match the originals.
