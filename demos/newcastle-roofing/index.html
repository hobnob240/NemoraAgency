Repair first. Replace only when it's needed.

Most leaks start somewhere small. Before anyone talks about a new roof, these are the things we check.

  • Tiles

    Slipped, cracked or missing tiles let water straight in. Often one or two is all it takes.

  • Lead flashing and soakers

    Where the roof meets a chimney or wall. Worn lead here is one of the most common causes of a leak.

  • Cement and pointing

    Old cement on ridges and verges cracks and falls away. Replacing it keeps the weather out.

  • Gutters

    Blocked or leaking gutters send water where it shouldn't go. We repair them or fit new.

  • Barge boards

    The boards along the gable edge. We'll source ones that match the originals.

  • The whole roof

    If it really is time for a new roof, we'll tell you straight and show you why.
