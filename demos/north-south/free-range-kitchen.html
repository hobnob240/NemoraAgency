How the kitchen cooks

Seasonal

The menu moves with the seasons, so dishes change as ingredients come and go.

Sustainable

It backs producers close to home and a local food system in North Yorkshire.

Foraged

Foraging sets the pace, and what is growing nearby gives each menu its ideas.

Open fire

Much of the cooking happens over flame, for flavour you can't get any other way.
