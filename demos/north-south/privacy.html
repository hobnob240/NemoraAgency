Privacy and cookies

This website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies, and it doesn't ask you for any personal details.

Who we are

North South, 25a Bootham, York YO30 7BW. Contact us at contactnorthsouth@gmail.com or on 01904 345110.

Cookies

We don't set cookies. The map on the Visit us page stays switched off until you press Show the map. After that, Google may set its own cookies under its privacy policy.

Booking and contacting us

Table bookings are taken through OrderTab, which handles your details under its own privacy policy. If you email or call us, we use your details only to reply and to manage your booking or event, and we don't share them for marketing.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email us and we'll reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
