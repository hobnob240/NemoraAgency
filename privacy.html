Privacy notice

How NEMORA handles personal information on this website.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by NEMORA. You can contact us about privacy at daniel@nemora.uk.

What we collect

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it doesn’t store anything you type into it. The contact form opens your own email app with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

When you email us, we receive your name, email address and anything else you choose to include, such as your phone number and details about your business. We use this only to reply to you and, if you go ahead, to provide our services. Our lawful basis is your request for information and, where relevant, our contract with you.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 2 years, or longer if you become a client and the law requires us to keep business records.

Who sees it

Only us. Our email is provided by our email service provider, which processes messages on our behalf. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it, by emailing us. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
