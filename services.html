Questions

Questions people ask us

How long does a website take?

Most sites go live within around two weeks once we have your content: your logo, photos and the details you want on the site. Larger builds take longer, and we’ll give you a timeline before we start.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Every plan covers hosting, your domain, SSL, security and urgent fixes. Business (£50 a month) adds up to 1 hour of changes every month, and Premium (£70 a month) adds up to 2 hours plus regular checks on bookings and payments. On Starter (£25 a month), content changes are quoted before we do them.

Do I pay everything upfront?

No. Half of your package’s setup fee is paid upfront to book your project, and the rest when your website is ready to go live.

Which package do I need?

Starter (£400, then £25 a month) suits a business that needs one professional page. Business (£795, then £50 a month) gives you up to five pages and your own admin area to make changes from your phone. Premium (£1,600, then £70 a month) adds events or listings, booking requests and card payments. If you’re unsure, ask and we’ll recommend one honestly.

Who owns the domain?

We register and manage your domain as part of the plan. If you decide to leave after your minimum term, we can transfer it to you for a fee.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We regularly rebuild older or slow websites, keeping what works and fixing what doesn’t.

Will it work on phones?

Every site is designed for phones first, because that’s where most of your customers will see it.

What about bigger projects?

Booking systems, member areas and platforms like Book a Medium Night are priced individually. Tell us what you need and we’ll give you a clear quote.
