Questions from Stone businesses

Can you build a site for a small shop or café in Stone?

Yes. Our Starter package (£400, then £25 a month) is one well-designed page with your opening times, location, photos and contact buttons, which is often all a small high street business needs.

Can I update it myself?

On the Business package you get your own admin area, so you can change prices, text and photos from your phone. On Starter, we make the changes for you.

How much does a website cost in Stone?

Our prices are the same everywhere and published up front: Starter £400 (then £25 a month), Business £795 (then £50 a month) and Premium £1,600 (then £70 a month). 12-month minimum term, half the setup fee upfront.

How long will it take?

Most sites go live within around two weeks once we have your logo, photos and details.
