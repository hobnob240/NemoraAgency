Recent websites and what each business needed

A selection of recent web design projects, from Stoke-on-Trent to Wolverhampton: what each business needed, and what we built for them.

Spitfire Comedy

Kind
Entertainment
Where
Stoke-on-Trent
Website
spitfirecomedy.co.uk (opens in a new tab)

A bold new website for a stand-up comedy club in Stoke-on-Trent, themed around its Spitfire plane logo. The next show sits up front on a “boarding pass”, with tickets one tap away.

What we built

  • Upcoming shows with a “boarding pass” card for the next night
  • Pages for the acts, open mic nights, reviews and venue bookings
  • Password-protected admin to update shows and photos
  • Technical search fixes so Google indexes the site properly

Where

A website for an events company that runs evenings of mediumship at venues around Stoke-on-Trent. Visitors can see what’s on, where, and what a night involves, then get in touch in a tap.

What we built

  • Event listings with venues and dates
  • Pages for the mediums, the evening itself and FAQs
  • Password-protected admin so the owner can update shows and photos from a phone
  • Click-to-call and mobile-first design

Book a Medium Night

Kind
Platform
Where
UK-wide
Website
Not on its own domain yet

Our own venture: a two-sided platform that connects pubs, clubs and venues with psychics and mediums. Venues list dates for free; mediums pay an annual membership to find bookings.

What we built

  • Venue registration with private edit links
  • Member accounts, a members’ portal and card payments through Stripe
  • Public profiles, “Find a medium” search and an enquiries inbox
  • Admin console, demo mode and UK GDPR tools built in
